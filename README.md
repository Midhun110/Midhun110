- 🎯 **Personalized Fitness Plans:** Tailored workout routines based on individual fitness goals.
- 📊 **Progress Dashboard:** Comprehensive visual analytics for physical gains and consistency.

### 🪚 Carpenter Expense Tracker
> **Real-World Business & Expense Management Application**

A tailored business management solution engineered for carpenter businesses to efficiently digitize customer records, track material expenses, manage worker payments, and automatically generate PDF invoices.

- 👥 **Customer Management:** Centralized database for client details, job status, and history.
- 🧱 **Material Expense Tracking:** Real-time logging of lumber, hardware, and job-specific expenditures.
- 👷 **Worker Payment Ledger:** Detailed tracking of daily labor costs and wage distributions.
- 📄 **PDF Invoice Generation:** Instant automated creation and export of professional PDF invoices.
- 📱 **Mobile-Friendly UI:** Optimized for quick, intuitive navigation on mobile devices.

**Tech Stack:** `React Native` • `JavaScript` • `Tailwind CSS` • `PDF Generation`
